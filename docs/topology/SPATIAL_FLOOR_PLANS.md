# Vertical Spatial Campus Floor Plans

REALNT HQ organizes enterprise campuses into a 5-floor vertical hierarchy. Each floor specializes in specific interaction patterns, density tolerances, and security boundaries.

---

## Campus Level Overview

| Level | Floor Designation | Primary Function | Max Concurrent Capacity | Default Privacy Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Ground Commons & Town Hall | Public reception, all-hands, casual cafe | 250 avatars | Open / Public |
| **Level 2** | Engineering & Systems Hub | Focus pods, sprint clusters, war rooms | 150 avatars | Team-Restricted |
| **Level 3** | Product, Design & Research | Design studios, research labs, roadmap | 100 avatars | Team-Restricted |
| **Level 4** | Revenue & Client Experience | Demo suites, pitch hubs, deal rooms | 100 avatars | Client / Semi-Public |
| **Level 5** | Executive & People Sanctum | Boardroom, 1-on-1s, governance | 50 avatars | Confidential / E2EE |

---

## Detailed Floor Layouts

### Level 1: Ground Commons & Town Hall
* **Visitor Reception Lobby**: Guarded perimeter zone where external visitors holding temporary passes (`REALNT-GUEST-XXXX`) materialize.
* **All-Hands Auditorium**: High-capacity amphitheater supporting stage broadcasts with speaker queues, presentation screens, and attendee chat.
* **Watercooler Lounge & Pantry**: Spatial proximity audio zone with distance attenuation, fostering serendipitous hallway conversations.
* **Standup Kiosk & Bulletin Wall**: Interactive terminal for posting asynchronous daily briefings and reading company-wide announcements.

### Level 2: Engineering & Systems Hub
* **Sprint Pod Desks**: Desk clusters grouped into 6-to-8 workstation pods for focused squad co-working.
* **Deep-Work Isolation Pods**: Acoustic booths equipped with Pomodoro timers, visual focus countdowns, and automatic knock buffering.
* **Production Incident War Room**: 12-seat emergency conference room connected to automated monitoring webhooks.
* **Architecture Scratchpad Hub**: Room featuring persistent whiteboard canvases with 1-click export to ADR decision registers.

### Level 3: Product, Design & Research Floor
* **Design Critique Studio**: Wide-canvas studio with Figma screen-share priority and participant pointer tracking.
* **User Research Observation Lab**: Dual-chamber room allowing interviewers and test participants to converse while observers remain muted.
* **Async Roadmap Wall**: Interactive board where product initiatives and architectural decision records are reviewed.

### Level 4: Revenue & Client Experience Floor
* **Customer Demo Suites**: Polished, branded rooms configured for client-facing pitch presentations.
* **Outbound SDR Call Hub**: Hot-desk grid equipped with individual active-call status indicators.
* **Deal Closing War Room**: Multi-party negotiation chamber with strict access lists and encrypted meeting notes.

### Level 5: Executive & People Sanctum Floor
* **Boardroom**: Executive chamber restricted to Tier 0 and Tier 1 roles with mandatory access logging.
* **Confidential HR 1-on-1 Booths**: Acoustic isolation rooms where soft knocks are disabled and door status reflects "Private Session".
* **Executive Suites**: Dedicated founder and director workspaces with custom broadcast presence messages.
