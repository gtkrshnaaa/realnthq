# Vertical Spatial Campus Floor Plans

REALNT HQ organizes enterprise campuses into a 7-level vertical hierarchy across 4 cardinal wings per floor. Each floor specializes in specific interaction patterns, density tolerances, and security boundaries.

For horizontal wing layouts, coordinate grids, and acoustic zoning, consult [VERTICAL_HORIZONTAL_HQ_MAPS.md](./VERTICAL_HORIZONTAL_HQ_MAPS.md).

## Detailed Floor Layouts

### Level B1: Subterranean Systems Vault
* **Infrastructure Monitoring Bay**: Real-time server telemetry consoles, Prometheus metrics, and distributed tracing monitors.
* **Cold Storage Archives**: Long-term persistent artifact backups and compliance audit log registries.
* **Network Operations Center (NOC)**: Live WebSocket connection load maps and cluster health indicators.

### Level 1: Ground Commons & Town Hall
* **Visitor Reception Lobby**: Guarded perimeter zone where external visitors holding temporary passes (`REALNT-GUEST-XXXX`) materialize.
* **All-Hands Auditorium**: High-capacity amphitheater supporting stage broadcasts with speaker queues, presentation screens, and attendee chat.
* **Watercooler Lounge & Pantry**: Spatial proximity audio zone with distance attenuation, fostering serendipitous hallway conversations.
* **Standup Kiosk & Bulletin Wall**: Interactive terminal for posting asynchronous daily briefings and reading company-wide announcements.

### Level 2: Engineering & Systems Hub
* **West Wing (Focus)**: Sprint desk clusters and Deep-Work Isolation Pods equipped with Pomodoro timers and buffered knock trays.
* **East Wing (Collaboration)**: 12-seat Production Incident War Room and Architecture Scratchpad Hub with 1-click ADR export.
* **Central Atrium**: Elevator banks and squad wayfinding terminals.
* **South Wing**: Coffee bar and pair-programming booths.

### Level 3: Product, Design & Research Floor
* **Design Critique Studio**: Wide-canvas studio with Figma screen-share priority and participant pointer tracking.
* **User Research Observation Lab**: Dual-chamber room allowing interviewers and test participants to converse while observers remain muted.
* **Async Roadmap Wall**: Interactive board where product initiatives and architectural decision records are reviewed.

### Level 4: Revenue & Client Experience Floor
* **Customer Demo Suites**: Polished, branded rooms configured for client-facing pitch presentations with visitor pass support.
* **Outbound SDR Call Hub**: Hot-desk grid equipped with individual active-call status indicators.
* **Deal Closing War Room**: Multi-party negotiation chamber with strict access lists and encrypted meeting notes.

### Level 5: People Ops & Workplace Culture Floor
* **Confidential HR 1-on-1 Booths**: Acoustic isolation rooms where soft knocks are disabled and door status reflects "Private Session".
* **Candidate Interview Suites**: Soundproof chambers configured for technical screenings and culture interviews.
* **People Ops Central Hub**: Team directory management desks and wellness sync stations.

### Level 6: Penthouse Boardroom & Founders Sanctum
* **Boardroom**: Formal governance chamber restricted to Tier 0 and Tier 1 roles with mandatory access logging.
* **Investor Observatory**: Panorama suite for strategic stakeholder briefings with encrypted screen feeds.
* **Founders Sanctum**: Dedicated workspace for company officers with Ghost Mode and priority broadcast controls.
