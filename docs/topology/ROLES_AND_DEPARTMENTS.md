# Departmental Taxonomy & Role Profiles

REALNT HQ structures enterprise organizations across 7 distinct departments and 25+ functional role profiles, ensuring operational needs are met beyond standard developer workflows.

---

## 1. Executive Leadership & Governance

* **Primary Roles**: Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Chief Technology Officer (CTO), Chief Operating Officer (COO), Chief Financial Officer (CFO), General Counsel.
* **Operational Needs**: High-confidentiality governance, board sessions, investor pitches, performance reviews, and external legal audits.
* **Spatial Configuration**:
  * Located primarily on Level 5 (Executive Sanctum).
  * Equipped with "Ghost Mode" (viewing workspace status without broadcasting presence).
  * Direct access to encrypted boardrooms with disabled recording flags.

---

## 2. Product, Engineering & Infrastructure

* **Primary Roles**: Engineering Managers, Distributed Systems Leads, Frontend/Backend Engineers, SRE/DevOps, QA Automation Engineers, Technical Product Managers.
* **Operational Needs**: Deep uninterrupted focus, pairing syncs, architecture design, and incident triage.
* **Spatial Configuration**:
  * Located primarily on Level 2 (Engineering Floor).
  * Dedicated sprint desk clusters partitioned by microservice domain.
  * Deep-Work Pods with active Pomodoro countdowns and buffered knock trays.
  * Production War Rooms hooked to incident management webhooks.

---

## 3. Product Design & User Research

* **Primary Roles**: VP of Design, Staff Product Designers, Design Systems Engineers, User Researchers, UX Writers.
* **Operational Needs**: Design critiques, visual spec handoffs, prototype walkthroughs, and user testing sessions.
* **Spatial Configuration**:
  * Located primarily on Level 3 (Product & Design Floor).
  * Design Critique Studios featuring high-resolution screen-sharing priority.
  * User Research Observation Labs with sandboxed external candidate access.

---

## 4. Revenue, Sales & Solutions Engineering

* **Primary Roles**: VP of Sales, Enterprise Account Executives (AEs), Sales Development Reps (SDRs), Solutions Architects, Deal Desk Specialists.
* **Operational Needs**: Client pitches, technical demonstrations, contract negotiations, and outbound call blitzes.
* **Spatial Configuration**:
  * Located primarily on Level 4 (Revenue & GTM Floor).
  * Client Demo Suites equipped with visitor magic links and branded hospitality banners.
  * SDR call booths with integrated CRM availability signals.

---

## 5. Marketing, Brand & Communications

* **Primary Roles**: Chief Marketing Officer (CMO), Content Strategists, Developer Advocates, Event Producers, PR Managers.
* **Operational Needs**: Product launch coordination, public broadcast streaming, community events, and company announcements.
* **Spatial Configuration**:
  * Shared presence between Level 1 (Auditorium/Commons) and Level 4.
  * Permissions to publish global news items to the Company Bulletin Portal.
  * Stage management controls in the Level 1 All-Hands Auditorium.

---

## 6. Customer Success & Support Operations

* **Primary Roles**: Head of Customer Success, Technical Account Managers (TAMs), Customer Support Engineers (Tiers 1-3), Escalation Managers.
* **Operational Needs**: Live escalation triage, shared client logs, shift handovers, and post-mortems.
* **Spatial Configuration**:
  * Shared presence on Level 2 and Level 4.
  * Support triage hotlines with live queue counters.
  * Persistent Markdown Decision Registers for client post-mortems.

---

## 7. People Operations, HR & Talent Acquisition

* **Primary Roles**: Head of People, Technical Recruiters, HR Business Partners, Employee Experience Leads.
* **Operational Needs**: Candidate screening, confidential 1-on-1 performance reviews, employee onboarding, and culture initiatives.
* **Spatial Configuration**:
  * Located on Level 5 and Level 1 (Reception).
  * Confidential acoustic isolation booths where soft knocks are disabled by default.
  * Visitor escort controls for interview candidate reception.
